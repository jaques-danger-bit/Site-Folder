Client Check-In

Weekly check-in.

Check-ins are due NLT 11:00 AM unless previously discussed with coach. Submit your check-in on time, in full, and with the requested photos. Consistency creates usable data. Usable data drives accurate adjustments.

Photo Guidelines

  • Take photos in the same spot every week.
  • Take them first thing in the morning, fasted.
  • Face natural light. Place phone on a window sill if possible.
  • Press record on video, use back camera, then screenshot stills from the video.

Clothing & Poses

  • Men: shirtless, shorts, front/back double bicep pose, or stage suit and division mandatories for competitors.
  • Women: sports bra, shorts, bikini, front/back bicep pose, or stage suit and division mandatories for competitors.
  • Keep clothing, lighting, angles, and poses the same every week.
